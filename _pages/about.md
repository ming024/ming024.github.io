I am a 5th-year Ph.D. student at [Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago (TTIC)](https://www.ttic.edu), advised by [Karen Livescu](https://home.ttic.edu/~klivescu/). My overarching goal is to create **Trustworthy, User-Friendly, and Human-Like Voice AI**. My current research focuses on two key directions:

- **Balancing Intelligence with Interactivity in Voice AI**  
How can we make full-duplex voice models more intelligent without compromising real-time interactivity? How can we better preserve the knowledge acquired during large-scale text pre-training? How should a voice model decide what to delegate, to whom, and when—whether to a larger language model, a text-based reasoning system, or external tools?
- **Aligning Speech Language Models (SLMs) with Human Preference**  
How can we align SLMs with human preferences to improve content fidelity, natural turn-taking, and emotional responsiveness? How to enhance overall user experience?

Aside from these, my past research in **Controllable Speech Generation**, **Efficient Fine-Tuning of SLMs**, **Flow-Matching & Diffusion Models**, and **Self-Supervised Speech Representations** has provided a strong technical foundation for pursuing these goals.



Prior to joining TTIC, I completed my Master’s degree in Computer Science at [National Taiwan University (NTU)](https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/main.php), where I had the fortune to work with [Lin-shan Lee](https://speech.ee.ntu.edu.tw/previous_version/lslNew.htm) and [Hung-yi Lee](https://speech.ee.ntu.edu.tw/~hylee/index.php) at the [Speech Processing Lab](https://speech.ee.ntu.edu.tw). I also gained valuable practical experience through summer internships at [Amazon Alexa TTS Research](https://www.amazon.science/tag/text-to-speech), [FAIR (AI at Meta)](https://ai.meta.com), [NVIDIA NeMo Team](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/conv-ai/), and [Kyutai](https://kyutai.org/).
